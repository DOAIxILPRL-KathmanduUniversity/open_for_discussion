# Nepal Bhasa (Newar) Web Portals, Digital Media & Community Archives

## Overview
This catalog documents active online journalism portals, digital children's reading platforms, encyclopedic dumps, community literary weblogs, and audio-visual media broadcasting in Nepal Bhasa (Newari / `new`). These resources provide diverse contemporary and literary corpora for pretraining, speech recognition (ASR), and linguistic research.

---

## 1. Online News & Journalism Portals

These websites publish daily, weekly, and archival news articles, editorials, interviews, and cultural essays in standard Devanagari Nepal Bhasa HTML:

| Portal | URL / Endpoints | Content Description & Scope |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Lahana News (ल्हाना न्युज)** | [lahananews.com](https://www.lahananews.com/) | Leading online daily newspaper in Nepal Bhasa. Rich in political commentary, local governance news, cultural events, and searchable ePaper issues. High lexical diversity. |
| **Nepalbhasa Times (नेपालभाषा टाइम्स)** | [nepalbhasatimes.com](https://nepalbhasatimes.com/) | Established daily newspaper covering municipal affairs, literary reviews (*बाखं*), cultural reports, and opinion pieces. |
| **NepalMandal (नेपालमण्डल अनलाइन)** | [nepalmandal.com](http://www.nepalmandal.com/) | Independent online portal featuring extensive archives across *मू बुखँ* (Top News), *बुखँ* (News, 580+ pages), *च्वसु* (Essays, 170+ pages), and literature (*चिनाखँ*, *बाखं*). |
| **Newa Patrakar Rastriya Dabu (नेवाः पत्रकार राष्ट्रिय दबू)** | [newapatrakar.org.np](https://www.newapatrakar.org.np/) | Official digital organ of the National Federation of Newar Journalists. Covers mother-tongue journalism workshops and municipal cultural bulletins. |
| **Subhay Post (सुभाय् पोष्ट)** | [subhaypost.com](https://subhaypost.com/) | Community news, socio-cultural updates, and local Kathmandu Valley heritage reporting in vernacular Nepal Bhasa. |
| **Ratopati (नेपालभाषा ब्व)** | [ratopati.com](https://ratopati.com/) | Major mainstream digital portal with dedicated coverage of Nepal Bhasa literary conferences (*तःमुँज्या*), language rights, and policy updates. |
| **Maitri News** | [maitrinews.com](https://maitrinews.com/) | Bilingual network publishing news, education initiatives (including *येँ देय् म्हसीके* curriculum), and opinion pieces. |
| **Inap Online (इनाप)** | [inaponline.com](https://inaponline.com/) | Historic weekly Newar publication providing news analysis, civic columns, and community editorials. |

## 2. Interactive Digital Reading Platforms & Children's Storybooks

## 3. Open Encyclopedias & Wikimedia Database Dumps

Structured, large-scale text dumps for entity definitions and historical narratives:

| Resource | URL / Access Link | Corpus Description |
| :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Nepal Bhasa Wikipedia** | [new.wikipedia.org](https://new.wikipedia.org/) | Over 70,000 articles in Nepal Bhasa covering history, geography, world politics, arts, religion, and biographies. Primary source of ASR formal sentences. |
| **Wikimedia Database Dumps (`newwiki`)** | [dumps.wikimedia.org/newwiki/](https://dumps.wikimedia.org/newwiki/) | Raw XML/SQL dumps of the entire Nepal Bhasa Wikipedia database updated bi-weekly. Clean raw text source for language model pretraining. |
| **Wikidata Entity & Lexeme Dumps** | [dumps.wikimedia.org](https://dumps.wikimedia.org/wikidatawiki/entities/) | Structured JSON entity dumps containing Nepal Bhasa labels, descriptions, aliases, and lexicographical lemmas (`new` lexemes with grammatical gender and cases). |
| **Newa Gyankosh (नेवाः सांस्कृतिक ज्ञानकोश)** | [newagyankosh.org](https://newagyankosh.org/) | Digital cultural encyclopedia providing detailed semantic entries for Newar rituals, folklore (*«पुनखुं मैंचा»*), indigenous architecture, and traditional festivals. |

## 4. Lexical, Cultural & Academic Digital Repositories

| **NepalMandal Cultural Archive** | [nepalmandal.com](http://www.nepalmandal.com/) | Cultural repository archiving ancient narratives, festival protocols (*मोहनी*, *स्वन्ति*), kite traditions (*भुतमालि*), and classical manuscripts (*स्वस्थानी बाखं*, *तन्त्राख्यान*). |

| **Laykoo Magazine Archive** | [vajracharya.wordpress.com](https://vajracharya.wordpress.com/) | Digitized issues of *Laykoo* (लाय्कू), the historic Nepal Bhasa literary and research journal featuring in-depth scholarly essays and cultural linguistics. |
