# NAAMII (Nepal Applied Mathematics and Informatics Institute for Research)

## Overview
**NAAMII** is an independent, non-profit research institution dedicated to democratizing AI and machine learning research in Nepal and developing nations.

## Research Areas
- Natural Language Processing for low-resource languages (specifically Nepali and Maithili).
- Computer Vision for healthcare and heritage archiving.
- Annual Nepal Winter School in AI.

## Key Projects & Models
- [NepaliBERT](wiki/models/nepalibert.md) & [NepBERTa](wiki/models/nepberta.md): Foundational transformer-based language models adapted for low-resource Devanagari representation.
- **Nepal Winter School in AI (NWSAI)**: Annual intensive training and research symposium connecting global AI researchers with Nepali graduate students.

## Founders & Principal Investigators
- **Dr. Binod Bhattarai**: Co-founder and research scientist; focuses on computer vision, deep representation learning, and natural language modeling.
- **Dr. Danda Pani Paudel**: Co-founder; senior researcher at ETH Zurich focusing on 3D computer vision and AI foundations.
- **Dr. Bishesh Khanal**: Co-founder and Director; specialist in medical imaging, biomedical AI, and ethical technology deployment.
