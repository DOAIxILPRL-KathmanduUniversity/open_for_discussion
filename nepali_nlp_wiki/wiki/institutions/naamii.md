# NAAMII (Nepal Applied Mathematics and Informatics Institute for Research)

## Overview
**NAAMII** is an independent, non-profit research institution dedicated to democratizing AI and machine learning research in Nepal and developing nations.

## Research Areas
- Natural Language Processing for low-resource languages (specifically Nepali and Maithili).
- Computer Vision for healthcare and heritage archiving.
- Annual Nepal Winter School in AI.
