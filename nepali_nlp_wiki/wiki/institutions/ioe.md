# Institute of Engineering (IOE), Tribhuvan University

## Overview
The **Institute of Engineering (IOE)**, established under Tribhuvan University, is Nepal's pioneer engineering education and research body. Its Department of Electronics and Computer Engineering (at Pulchowk Campus and constituent campuses) is the most prolific academic source of undergraduate and graduate research in Nepali computational linguistics, speech processing, and Devanagari OCR.

## Primary Research Publications & Conferences
- **IOE Graduate Conference (IOEGC)**: Semi-annual national conference showcasing peer-reviewed research in Nepali NLP (speech emotion detection, Transformer headline generation, Wav2Vec2 fine-tuning, RAG for legal documents, and fake news detection).
- **Journal of the Institute of Engineering (JIE)**: Peer-reviewed journal indexing foundational studies in speech recognition, deep learning, and image super-resolution.
- **Central Library Digital Repository (eLibrary TUCL)**: Houses master's theses and doctoral dissertations on Nepali morphology, syntax parsing, and neural machine translation.

## Key Faculty & Active NLP Supervisors
- **Dr. Basanta Joshi**: Associate Professor at Pulchowk Campus; prolific supervisor in speech synthesis (FastSpeech 2, MelGAN), end-to-end ASR, and speech emotion recognition.
- **Dr. Aman Shakya**: Associate Professor; supervisor for word embeddings, semantic representation, and language modeling.
- **Dr. Arun Kumar Timalsina**: Faculty researcher in document classification, Nepali news trend analysis, and Vision Transformers for handwritten character recognition.
- **Dr. Surendra Shrestha & Dr. Sanjeeb Prasad Panday**: Key supervisors across information retrieval, clickbait detection, and automated fake news detection.
