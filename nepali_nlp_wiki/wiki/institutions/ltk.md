# Language Technology Kendra (LTK)

## Overview
**Language Technology Kendra (LTK)** is a pioneering non-profit organization established to foster information technology localization for Nepali and other indigenous languages of Nepal. 

## Key Contributions
- Development of the **Nepali Spell Checker**, electronic thesaurus, and localized dictionary tools.
- Leadership in international localization projects including PAN Localization.
- Standardization of Unicode keyboard layouts and phonetic transliteration tools for Devanagari.
- Creation of **NepaLinux**: A pioneering localized operating system distribution (based on Debian and GNOME) with complete Nepali system interfaces and open-source office suites.

## Institutional Collaboration
- **Madan Puraskar Pustakalaya (MPP)**: Close historical partnership with Nepal's premier non-governmental archive for archiving and digitizing national cultural heritage.
- **UNESCO & IDRC Support**: Co-funded multi-country research initiatives addressing South Asian language enablement in cyberspace.
