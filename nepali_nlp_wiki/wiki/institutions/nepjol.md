# Nepal Journals Online (NepJOL)

## Overview
**Nepal Journals Online (NepJOL)** is Nepal's official open-access academic publishing platform, initiated in partnership with INASP and operated by Tribhuvan University Central Library. It provides permanent digital object identifiers (DOIs) and full-text archiving for hundreds of academic journals published in Nepal.

## Role in Nepali NLP & AI Research
NepJOL serves as the primary indexed repository for domestic computer science and engineering research:
- **Hosted Technical Journals**: *Journal of the Institute of Engineering (JIE)*, *Technical Journal*, *KEC Journal of Science and Engineering (KJSE)*, *Journal of Advanced College of Engineering and Management (JACEM)*, *Journal of Himalaya College of Engineering (JHCoE)*, *Nepalese Linguistics*, and *Gipan*.
- **Literature Coverage**: Spans classical rule-based Nepali grammar analyzers to modern deep learning implementations (mBART summarization, Whisper transcription, Devanagari character recognition, and differential privacy sentiment models).
