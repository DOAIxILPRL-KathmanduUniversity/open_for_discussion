# Nepal Open Data & Government Statistical Portals

In addition to linguistic text corpora and speech audio, empirical computational social science, NLP tabular question-answering, and socio-linguistic analysis in Nepal require structured numerical and administrative datasets. This node compiles key government open data portals, disaster records, economic indicators, and civic repositories.

---

## 1. Central Statistical & Demographic Repositories

### National Statistics Office (NSO / Central Bureau of Statistics - CBS)
- **Portal**: [National Statistics Office](https://censusnepal.cbs.gov.np/)
- **Data Domains**: National Population and Housing Census (2011, 2021), National Agriculture Census, Economic Censuses, Living Standard Surveys (NLSS).
- **Format**: Microdata (STATA, SPSS, CSV), statistical yearbooks, district/municipality-level demographic indicators.

### Open Data Nepal
- **Portal**: [Open Data Nepal](https://opendatanepal.com/)
- **Description**: Civil-society driven open data catalog aggregating thousands of datasets across education, health, election results, infrastructure, and budget expenditures.

---

## 2. Macroeconomic & Financial Portals

### Nepal Rastra Bank (NRB) Data Portal
- **Portal**: [NRB Economic Data Portal](https://www.nrb.org.np/)
- **Data Domains**: Foreign exchange rates, inflation rates, CPI indices, remittance inflows, banking sector assets, balance of payments.
- **Access**: Historical daily/monthly tables downloadable in Excel/CSV.

### Ministry of Finance (MoF) Open Budget Portals
- **Portal**: [MoF Nepal](https://www.mof.gov.np/)
- **Data Domains**: Annual Budget Speeches (Devanagari), Line Ministry Budget Information System (LMBIS), Red Book allocations, revenue collection reports.

---

## 3. Disaster Management & Hydrometeorology

### DesInventar Nepal
- **Portal**: [DesInventar Profile: Nepal](https://www.desinventar.net/)
- **Coverage**: Historical spatial database of disaster events (1971–present) culled from *Gorkhapatra* and *Kantipur* newspapers, documenting floods, landslides, fires, earthquakes, and casualties.

### National Emergency Operation Center (NEOC)
- **Portal**: [NEOC Disaster Portal](http://neoc.gov.np/)
- **Data Domains**: Incident reports, disaster loss figures, monsoon hazard updates, daily emergency situation reports.

### Department of Hydrology and Meteorology (DHM) Flood & Weather Watch
- **Portal**: [DHM Real-Time Water Level & Rainfall](http://hydrology.gov.np/)
- **Data Domains**: Automated river-basin telemetry stations, hourly rainfall gauges, flood warning bulletins.

---

## 4. Aviation & Transport Safety

### Aviation Safety Network (ASN) - Nepal Air Safety Profile
- **Portal**: [Aviation Safety Network Nepal](https://aviation-safety.net/database/dblist.php?Country=9N)
- **Coverage**: Comprehensive verified registry of civil and military airliner accidents, incident reports, flight paths, and airworthiness investigations in Nepalese airspace.

---

## 5. Electoral & Civic Records

### Election Commission of Nepal (ECN)
- **Portal**: [ECN Results Portal](https://result.election.gov.np/)
- **Coverage**: Local, provincial, and federal election candidate rosters, voting tallies, voter turnout percentages, and constituency demographics.
