# Nepali Text Classification Research Literature

## Overview
Text classification represents the most widely studied supervised NLP benchmark in Nepali, serving as the standard testbed for evaluating new tokenizers, TF-IDF schemes, and neural representations.

## Key Publications

- **Acharya et al. (2020)**: *An Analysis of Classification Algorithms for Nepali News*.
  - *Benchmark*: Evaluated Naive Bayes, Multinomial Logistic Regression, and SVM on the 16NepaliNews dataset, noting the heavy dependence of classification accuracy on morphological stemmer quality.
- **Kafle et al. (2022)**: *Improving Nepali News Classification Using Bidirectional Encoder Representation from Transformers (BERT)*.
  - *Benchmark*: Demonstrated significant performance leaps using transformer architectures over BiLSTM and static word vectors.
- **Sitaula et al. (2021)**: *Vector representation based on a supervised codebook for Nepali documents classification*.
  - *Method*: Introduced codebook feature clusters to address high vocabulary sparseness in agglutinative Nepali text.
- **Dam et al. (2021)**: *Detecting Clickbaits on Nepali News using SVM and RF*.
  - *Application*: Classification of headline-body dissonance and sensationalism in Nepali digital portals.
