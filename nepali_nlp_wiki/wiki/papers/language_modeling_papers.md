# Nepali Language Modeling & Transformers Research Literature

## Overview
Self-supervised language models trained on massive Devanagari text corpora have largely supplanted static embeddings and shallow neural networks for downstream natural language understanding tasks in Nepali.

## Key Publications

- **Timilsina et al. (2022)**: *NepBERTa: Nepali Language Model Trained in a Large Corpus*.
  - *Contribution*: Pre-trained a RoBERTa-based model from scratch using Byte-Pair Encoding (BPE) on over 1.5GB of raw crawled Nepali text, establishing state-of-the-art results across text classification and NER.
- **Maskey et al. (2022)**: *Nepali Encoder Transformers: An Analysis of Auto Encoding Transformer Language Models for Nepali Text Classification*.
  - *Contribution*: Evaluated monolingual vs. multilingual encoders (mBERT, XLM-RoBERTa, NepaliBERT) under low-resource fine-tuning budgets.
- **Awale et al. (2022)**: *Preprocessing of Nepali News Corpus for Downstream Tasks*.
  - *Contribution*: Empirical analysis showing that rigorous Unicode normalization and stopword removal significantly improves masked language model convergence.
