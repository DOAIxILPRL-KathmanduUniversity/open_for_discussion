# Nepali Speech Processing Research Literature

## Overview
Speech synthesis and recognition for Nepali present challenges related to acoustic phonetic mapping, syllable segmentation, and prosodic phrasing for tonal/stress dynamics.

## Key Publications & Theses

### 1. Text-to-Speech (TTS)
- **Bajracharya et al. (2018)**: *Building a Natural Sounding Text-to-Speech System for the Nepali Language: Research and Development Challenges and Solutions*.
  - *Focus*: Unit selection synthesis, grapheme-to-phoneme (G2P) rule engineering, and handling conjuncts in acoustic synthesis.
- **Bal et al. (2017)**: *Enhancing the Quality of Nepali Text-to-Speech Systems*.
  - *Focus*: Pitch-synchronous overlap-and-add (PSOLA) and Epoch-Synchronous Non-Linear Overlap-Add (ESNOLA) methods for natural prosodic rhythm.
- **Paudel et al. (2023 - TU Report)**: *SHRUTI - A Nepali Book Reader*.
  - *Focus*: Neural end-to-end TTS integration for automated document reading for visually impaired users.

### 2. Automatic Speech Recognition (ASR)
- **Dongol et al. (2020)**: *Nepali Speech Recognition Using Convolutional Neural Networks (CNN)*.
  - *Focus*: Spectrogram feature extraction coupled with acoustic CNN classifiers for isolated and continuous Nepali words.
- **OpenSLR 54 Publication**: Documentation of acoustic data collection protocol across diverse regional accents across Nepal.
