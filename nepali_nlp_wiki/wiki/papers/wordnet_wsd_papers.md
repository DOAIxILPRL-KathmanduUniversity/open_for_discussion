# Nepali WordNet & Word Sense Disambiguation Research Literature

## Overview
WordNet construction in Nepali follows the expansion approach (anchoring to Hindi/English synsets via IndoWordNet) and forms the backbone for addressing polysemy through Word Sense Disambiguation (WSD).

## Key Publications

- **Prasain et al. (2008)**: *Resources for Nepali Word Sense Disambiguation*.
  - *Contribution*: Early formulation of machine-readable dictionaries and annotated corpus resources for baseline Nepali word sense disambiguation, presented at IEEE NLP-KE 2008 ([DOI: 10.1109/nlpke.2008.4906758](https://doi.org/10.1109/nlpke.2008.4906758)).
- **Chakraborty et al. (2009 / 2011)**: *Experiences in Building the Nepali WordNet - Insights and Challenges*.
  - *Contribution*: Comprehensive documentation of building synsets, lexical relations, and ontology alignment for Nepali within the IndoWordNet initiative.
- **Dhungana et al. (2012 / 2014)**: *Word Sense Disambiguation using WSD Specific Wordnet of Polysemy Words & PolyWordNet*.
  - *Contribution*: Construction of domain-specific synsets and clue-word heuristic algorithms to resolve word polysemy in ambiguous contexts.
- **Singh et al. (2021)**: *Nepali Word-Sense Disambiguation Using Variants of Simplified Lesk Measure*.
  - *Contribution*: Evaluated overlap-based Lesk variants and synset gloss embeddings on Nepali ambiguous target words.
