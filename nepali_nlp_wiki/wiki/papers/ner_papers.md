# Nepali Named Entity Recognition (NER) Research Literature

## Overview
Because Devanagari lacks capital letters to signal proper nouns, Named Entity Recognition algorithms must rely on morphological suffixes, context gazetteers, and contextual subword embeddings.

## Key Publications

- **Bal et al. (2019)**: *Named Entity Recognition (NER) for Nepali*.
  - *Contribution*: Explored sequence labeling on national news corpora using Conditional Random Fields (CRF) and lexical gazetteers.
- **Singh et al. (2019)**: *Named Entity Recognition for Nepali Language*.
  - *Contribution*: Implemented BiLSTM-CRF networks to capture bidirectional sentence context without handcrafted morphology rules.
- **Dey et al. (2014)**: *Named Entity Recognition for Nepali language: A Semi Hybrid Approach*.
  - *Contribution*: Fused rule-based affix heuristics with Support Vector Machine (SVM) classifiers to handle out-of-vocabulary named entities.
