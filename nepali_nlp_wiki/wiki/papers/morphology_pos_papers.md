# Nepali Morphology, Stemming & POS Tagging Research Literature

## Overview
Morphological processing is the most critical prerequisite in the computational analysis of Nepali. Verbal inflections, nominal cases, and sandhi rules require specialized formal linguistic models.

## Key Publications & Theses

- **Bal Krishna Bal (2004)**: *Structure of Nepali Grammar & A Morphological Analyzer and Stemmer for Nepali*.
  - *Landmark*: The seminal computational morphology model establishing finite-state transducers (FST) and rule-based root extraction for Nepali vocabulary.
- **Dr. Balaram Prasain (2011 - University of Konstanz Dissertation)**: *A Computational Analysis of Nepali Morphology: A Model for Natural Language Processing*.
  - *Landmark*: Exhaustive formal linguistics dissertation providing the theoretical foundation for computational noun inflection, verb paradigms, and derivational affix morphology in Nepali.
- **Hardie et al. (2005 / 2009 / 2011)**: *Nelralec Tagset (NT-01) & Advances in Tokenisation and Lemmatisation*.
  - *Landmark*: Developed the standard 40+ category POS tagset specifically tailored for the syntactic nuances of contemporary Nepali.
- **Shrestha & Bal (2021)**: *Fine-grained part-of-speech tagging in Nepali text*.
  - *Contribution*: High-resolution tagset evaluation using neural classifiers to differentiate verbal aspects and auxiliary constructions.
