# Nepali Sentiment Analysis Research Literature

## Overview
Sentiment analysis in Nepali presents unique linguistic complexities: negation placement, aspect markers, figurative idioms, and mixed-code Romanized/Devanagari text on digital platforms.

## Key Publications

- **Amatya & Bal (2016)**: *Automatic Detection of Opinion Polarity and Their Strengths in Newspaper Editorials*.
  - *Method*: Combines rule-based dependency patterns with sentiment lexicons to score editorial subjectivity.
- **Bal & Saint-Dizier (2014)**: *Have we had Hard Times or Cosy Times? A Discourse Analysis of Opinions Expressed over Socio-political Events in News Editorials*.
  - *Method*: Discourse-level appraisal theory applied to Nepali political prose.
- **Subba et al. (2020)**: *Sentiment Analysis in Nepali: Exploring Machine Learning and Lexicon-Based Approaches*.
  - *Method*: Empirical comparison of Naive Bayes, Support Vector Machines (SVM), and Random Forests against SentiWordNet adaptations for Nepali.
- **Adhikari et al. (2021)**: *Aspect-Based Sentiment Analysis of Nepali Text Using Deep Learning*.
  - *Method*: BiLSTM with Attention mechanisms to pinpoint sentiment towards specific entities rather than document-level averaging.
