# Quantum NLP Foundations

> [!IMPORTANT] Gist
> Quantum computing leverages superposition and entanglement to solve problems that are intractable for classical computers. Quantum NLP (QNLP) explores how these principles can be used to represent language meaning and accelerate neural network training (Ghayoumi, 2026).

## 1. Core Quantum Principles
- **Superposition**: A quantum bit (qubit) can exist in multiple states simultaneously ($|0\rangle$ and $|1\rangle$).
- **Entanglement**: Qubits can be linked such that the state of one instantly influences the state of another, regardless of distance.
- **Interference**: Using quantum gates to amplify correct answers and cancel out incorrect ones.

## 2. Quantum Machine Learning (QML)
- **Quantum Circuits**: Representing neural network layers as a series of quantum gates.
- **Quantum Speedup**: The potential for exponential speedup in optimization and large-scale search (e.g., Grover's algorithm).
- **Hybrid Classical-Quantum Models**: Using a classical computer for data preprocessing and a quantum processor for specific high-intensity sub-tasks.

## 3. Quantum Natural Language Processing (QNLP)
- **Mathematical Parallelism**: The compositional nature of quantum mechanics mirrors the compositional nature of language (Lambek calculus, Pregroup grammar).
- **DisCoCat Models**: Categorical Distributional Compositional models that map linguistic structures directly onto quantum circuits.
- **Representation**: Storing large semantic spaces in a logarithmic number of qubits.
