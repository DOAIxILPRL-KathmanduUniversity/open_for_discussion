# NLP in HCI & Augmented Reality

> [!IMPORTANT] Gist
> Natural language is the primary interface for Human-Computer Interaction (HCI) in ubiquitous and augmented environments. Voice assistants and gesture-based NLP enable seamless interaction in "Software-Defined" environments (Raj et al., 2024).

## 1. Ubiquitous Computing (UC)
- **Definition**: Computing that is "everywhere" and "disappearing" into the environment.
- **Layers**: Sensors, communication, and cognitive computing.
- **Wearables**: Devices like Apple Watch, Fitbit, and Smart Clothes that use NLP for health monitoring and user alerts.

## 2. Augmented Reality (AR) in HCI
- **Superimposition**: Overlaying digital information onto the physical world (e.g., Pokémon Go, HoloLens).
- **Voice-Driven AR**: Using voice commands to manipulate AR objects or retrieve information while the user's hands are busy (e.g., in military or architectural training).
- **Eye-Tracking**: Stabilizing and controlling interfaces using pupil movement, often integrated with voice for "Look-and-Speak" interaction.

## 3. Voice Assistants as HMI
- **Landmarks**: Apple's Siri, Amazon's Alexa, and Google Assistant.
- **Functionality**: Moving from simple command-response to proactive, context-aware agents.
