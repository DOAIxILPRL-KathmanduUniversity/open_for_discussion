# NLP & Blockchain

> [!IMPORTANT] Gist
> The integration of Blockchain and NLP focuses on data integrity, decentralized knowledge markets, and automated legal compliance through smart contract analysis (Agrawal et al., 2025).

## 1. Decentralized Knowledge Markets
- Using blockchain to track and reward high-quality data contributions for low-resource languages.
- Ensuring data provenance and authenticity in the era of synthetic AI content.

## 2. Secure Information Retrieval (IR)
- **Business Industry Synergy**: Leveraging blockchain's immutable blocks (Hash of previous block, current info, hash of upcoming block) to store and share data securely.
- **Intrusion Prevention**: Since blocks are interconnected via hashes, intruders cannot modify data without the user's knowledge, ensuring high-fidelity information retrieval (Sheela & Priya, 2021).
- **Decentralized AI Storage**: Using blockchain to manage the metadata and provenance of large-scale NLP datasets.

## 3. Challenges
- Latency and throughput constraints of decentralized ledgers.
- Data privacy vs. on-chain transparency.
