# Incongruous Text: Sarcasm, Metaphor & Humour

> [!IMPORTANT] Gist
> Traditional NLP focuses on the "most likely" interpretation. Incongruous NLP deals with non-literal language where the intended meaning differs significantly from the surface text (Bhattacharyya & Joshi, 2023).

## 1. Sarcasm & Satires
- **Incongruity**: Contrast between surface sentiment and negative context.
- **Social Media Challenges**: Sarcasm often uses colloquial words, emojis, and hashtags as modifiers. Detection requires analyzing the **intent** vs. the **explicit message**.
- **Metrics**: Standard classification metrics (Precision/Recall/F1) are used, but performance is often capped by the lack of cultural/situational context.

## 2. Metaphor Comprehension
- **Subjectivity & Emotions**: Recent models use **Ekman emotion categories** and pre-trained embeddings to model the subjectivity of the reader.
- **Cognitive Schemas**: Users prefer meanings that fit their existing cognitive schemas. Metaphors map a latent idea in a **target domain** to an evocative concept from a **source domain** (Rai et al., 2021).

## 3. Humour & Hate Content
- **Humour**: Relies on surprise shifts and sentiment incongruity.
- **Hate Content Detection**: A critical frontier where AI must distinguish between offensive speech, satire, and hate-motivated data based on religion, caste, or sex (Ghosal & Jain, 2021).
