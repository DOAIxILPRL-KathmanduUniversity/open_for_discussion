# Advanced Reasoning Architectures

> [!IMPORTANT] Gist
> Moving beyond simple linear inference, modern NLP employs iterative and collaborative loops to enhance logical depth and resolve complex ambiguities (2025/2026 Conference Insights).

## 1. Iterative Reasoning
- **Loop-of-Thought (LoT)**: Improving reasoning in Small Language Models (SLMs) by enabling iterative refinement of thought processes, moving from a "chain" to a "loop."
- **Dynamic Chain-of-Thought (D-CoT)**: Adjusting the depth of reasoning steps dynamically based on the complexity of the event extraction task.
- **Retrospective Replay**: Using past reasoning successes and failures to guide RL exploration for complex problem-solving.

## 2. Collaborative Reasoning (Multi-Agent Debate)
- **Multi-Agent Multi-Round Debate (MMD)**: Multiple specialized agents debate to solve Temporal Knowledge Graph Reasoning (TKGR) or legal charges.
- **MAD-HD (Hallucination Detection)**: A debate-driven framework where agents cross-examine outputs to detect ungrounded hallucinations.
- **Legal Charge Disambiguation**: Agents represent different legal perspectives to resolve confusing or overlapping charges in multi-party prediction datasets (MultiJustice).

## 3. Grounded Navigation
- **ReGA (Reasoning & Grounding Agents)**: Decoupling reasoning from execution in GUI navigation, allowing agents to "think" about the UI hierarchy before interacting with elements.
