# Corpus Linguistics & Stylistics

> [!IMPORTANT] Gist
> Corpus linguistics uses statistical analysis of large text collections to study language variation. **Stylistics** focuses on grammatical and lexical choices that reveal social attributes like dialect, gender, or individual authorship (Dunn, 2022).

## 1. Representing Style: Function Word N-grams
While content words (nouns/verbs) tell us *what* is being said, function words (pronouns, prepositions, conjunctions) tell us *how* it is being said.
- **Dialect Classification**: Grammatical choices (constructions) vary across regions.
- **Authorship Attribution**: Every writer has an unconscious "linguistic fingerprint" defined by their frequency of function word bigrams/trigrams.

## 2. Dialectology
The study of regional variations in language.
- **Syntactic Variation**: Alternations like "go around" vs "avoid" or "commute" vs "way to work" serve as sociolinguistic markers.
- **Prediction Accuracy**: Models using function words can achieve high precision in identifying the source city or author of a text.

## 3. Stylistic Variation & Context
- We change our language based on authority and context (e.g., email to a boss vs. an employee).
- Function words serve as a reliable proxy for these syntactic variations.
