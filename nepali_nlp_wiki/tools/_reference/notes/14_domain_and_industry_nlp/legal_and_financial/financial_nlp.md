# Financial NLP: Sentiment and Risk

> [!IMPORTANT] Gist
> In finance, NLP provides real-time decision support by analyzing market sentiment, detecting fraud, and managing regulatory compliance (Ranjan et al., 2025).

## 1. Market Sentiment Analysis
- **Sources**: News articles, social media (Reddit/Twitter), earnings reports.
- **Impact**: Rapid market movements are often driven by sentiment shifts detected by automated NLP pipelines.
- **Techniques**: Moving beyond simple polarity (positive/negative) to identifying nuanced entities and events.

## 2. Fraud and Anomaly Detection
- **Techniques**: Using word embeddings and contextual language models to identify patterns in transactions that deviate from the norm.
- **Financial Security**: Scanning financial documents for inconsistencies that indicate fraud.

## 3. Financial Inclusivity & Risk
- **KYC/AML**: "Know Your Customer" and "Anti-Money Laundering" checks.
- **Creditworthiness Assessment**: Quantifying risk for "unbanked" communities using alternative data (social media, e-commerce purchases, and internet usage) as digital footprints for repayment ability (Goyal & Saxena, 2021).
- **Contract Intelligence**: Automating the review of complex financial agreements for risk assessment.
