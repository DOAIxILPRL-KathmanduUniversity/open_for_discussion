# Digital Content Moderation & Information Disorders

> [!IMPORTANT] Gist
> Content moderation involves identifies and mitigating online harms such as hate speech, misinformation, and radicalization using a blend of NLP and sociological models (Kumar, 2025).

## 1. Information Disorders
- **Misinformation**: False information spread without malicious intent.
- **Disinformation**: Deliberately deceptive content.
- **Malinformation**: Truthful information used with malicious intent to cause harm.

## 2. Hate Speech & Cyberbullying
- **Detection Pipelines**: Monitoring for aggressive language, slurs, and context-dependent harassment.
- **Challenges**: Handling sarcasm, code-switching, and evolving internet slang.

## 3. Extremism & Radicalization
- **Identification**: Detecting linguistic patterns associated with extremist ideologies and recruitment tactics.
- **The Aarhus Model**: A framework for mitigating violent extremism through multi-agency collaboration and prevention-oriented interventions.
