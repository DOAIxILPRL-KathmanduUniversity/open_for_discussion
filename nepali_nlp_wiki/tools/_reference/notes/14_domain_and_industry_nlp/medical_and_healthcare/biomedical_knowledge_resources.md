# Biomedical Knowledge Resources

> [!IMPORTANT] Gist
> The biomedical domain is resource-rich, providing standardized terminologies and datasets for benchmarking clinical NLP systems (Demner-Fushman & Xu, 2024).

## 1. Unified Medical Language System (UMLS)
The UMLS is the primary resource for clinical NLP, consisting of three main sources:
- **Metathesaurus**: A huge database of biomedical concepts and their names in multiple languages.
- **Semantic Network**: A set of broad categories (Semantic Types) and their relationships (Semantic Relations).
- **SPECIALIST Lexicon**: A large syntactic lexicon of biomedical and general English words used for natural language processing (lemmatization, stemming).

## 2. Clinical Datasets
- **MIMIC (Medical Information Mart for Intensive Care)**: A freely available database of de-identified health data (version IV is the latest). It is essential for training and testing clinical information extraction models.
- **MIMIC-IV-Note**: Specifically contains de-identified free-text clinical notes for research.

## 3. Clinical Informatics Tools
- **MetaMap / MetaMap Lite**: Tools to map biomedical text to the UMLS Metathesaurus.
- **cTAKES (Mayo Clinical Text Analysis and Knowledge Extraction System)**: An open-source pipeline for information extraction from clinical narrative.
- **CLAMP**: A toolkit for building customized clinical NLP pipelines.
