# Medical NLP

> [!IMPORTANT] Gist
> NLP in medicine (Clinical NLP) enables the extraction and summarization of vital information—signs, symptoms, drug dosages, and side effects—from unstructured clinical reports.

## 1. Historical Systems
- **LSP-MLP (Linguistic String Project)**: One of the largest medical NLP projects; extracted drug responses and highlighted side effects.
- **Specialist System (NLM)**: Developed as an IE tool for Biomedical Knowledge Bases (Medline abstracts), using MeSH (Medical Subject Headings).
- **MEDLEE**: Columbia University's system for transforming narrative reports into structured representations.
- **RECIT**: A multilingual system using "Proximity Processing" to preserve language-independent medical knowledge.

## 2. Knowledge Resources
- **MeSH (Medical Subject Headings)**: A comprehensive controlled vocabulary for indexing journal articles.
- **UMLS (Unified Medical Language System)**: Integrates many different health and biomedical vocabularies.

## 3. Specialized Medical Extraction
- **Clinical Decision Support**: Flagging drug interactions or missing diagnoses.
- **Rheumatoid Arthritis (RA) Detection**: Utilizing **CNN and Reinforcement Learning** to analyze wrist/hand MRI narratives and images, achieving high precision in early stages (Krishnachalitha & Priya, 2021).
- **Chemical NER**: Extracting chemical names, formulas, and structures from literature using deep learning to distinguish between classification frameworks (Hema & Ajantha, 2021).
- **Bio-Surveillance**: Monitoring clinical feeds for disease outbreaks.
