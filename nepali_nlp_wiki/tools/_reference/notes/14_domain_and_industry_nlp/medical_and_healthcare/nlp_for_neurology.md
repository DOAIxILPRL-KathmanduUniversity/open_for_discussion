# NLP for Neurology & Forensic Applications

> [!IMPORTANT] Gist
> NLP and signal processing can restore lost speech and detect neurological conditions early by analyzing linguistic and acoustic features (Pillai & Tedesco, 2024).

## 1. Parkinson's Disease Detection
- **Feature Analysis**: Identifying linguistic "fingerprints" and acoustic markers (jitter, shimmer, vocal tremor) through ML approaches to detect early-stage Parkinson's.
- **Clinical Monitoring**: Using NLP to track cognitive-linguistic decline over time in longitudinal studies.

## 2. Speech Restoration & Forensics
- **VocalHUM / Whisper Restoration**: A smart system to enhance the intelligibility of whispered speech in real-time, reducing the patient's respiratory effort.
- **Source-Model Filtering**: Combining real speech with filtered models to restore clarity in clinical and forensic audio samples.

## 3. NLP in Psychotherapy
- **Dialogue Analysis**: Assisting psychotherapists by identifying emotional patterns and claim-check cycles in therapy sessions.
- **Claim Identification**: Helping clinicians track the progress of specific behavioral or cognitive claims made by the patient.
