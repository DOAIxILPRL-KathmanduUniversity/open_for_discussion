# NLP by Industry: Real-World Applications

> [!IMPORTANT] Gist
> NLP is transforming industry verticals by automating documentation, enhancing customer interaction through chatbots, and extracting insights from massive unstructured datasets (Singh, 2024).

## 1. Healthcare & Medicine
- **Clinical Records**: Summarizing patient histories and extracting Protected Health Information (PHI).
- **Diagnostics**: AI-based self-explanation assessments and medical language processors (LSP-MLP).

## 2. E-commerce & Retail
- **Product Search**: Handling typos and identifying attributes (size, color) from queries.
- **Customer Reviews**: Sentiment analysis and theme extraction from hotel/product reviews.
- **Chatbots**: 24/7 lead generation and ordering (e.g., pizza chatbots with RASA).

## 3. Finance & Legal
- **GDPR Compliance**: Automated document analysis to identify and secure sensitive data.
- **Market Sentiment**: Analyzing social media impact on stock prices.
- **Invoicing**: OCR-to-text conversion for automated accounts payable.

## 4. Hospitality & Real Estate
- **Hotel Review Analysis**: Categorizing unlabeled comments into "Service," "Location," and "Room" themes.
- **Property Listings**: Automatic information extraction from real estate descriptions.

## 5. Automotive & Supply Chain
- **Infotainment**: Natural language interfaces for vehicle control.
- **Procurement**: Chatbots for supply chain operations and vendor management.

## 6. Agriculture & Ecology
- **IAPS Detection**: Using Computer Vision and deep learning (YOLO, DenseNet) to identify Invasive Alien Plant Species (see [Agricultural Vision AI](agricultural_vision_ai.md)).
- **Feedback Loops**: Implementing human-in-the-loop systems for continuous retraining of field-deployed models.

## 7. Global Business & Localization
- **Market Orientation**: Using NLP to bridge the gap between healthcare databases (Pubmed) and search engines (Google) to understand therapies for **Rare Diseases**.
- **Computer-Assisted Translation (CAT)**: High-stakes business environments use **Quality Assurance (QA)** tools to detect translation memory errors.
    - **MQM Framework**: Multidimensional Quality Metrics used to categorize errors as Minor, Major, or Critical.
- **News Monitoring**: Deriving business intelligence from online news articles, social media messages, and user reviews using metrics extraction and fuzzy systems (Camilleri, 2021).
