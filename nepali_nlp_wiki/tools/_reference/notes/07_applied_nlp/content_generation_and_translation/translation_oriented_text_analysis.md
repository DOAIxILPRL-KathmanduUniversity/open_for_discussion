# Translation-Oriented Text Analysis

> [!IMPORTANT] Gist
> Translation-oriented text analysis is a functionalist approach where the source text (ST) is analyzed to understand its communicative function, which then guides the creation of a target text (TT) that fulfills a specific "Skopos" (purpose) in the target culture (Nord, 2005).

## 1. Skopos Theory
The Greek word *Skopos* means "purpose" or "goal." In translation:
- **The Skopos Rule**: The purpose of the translation determines the translation methods and strategies.
- **Intertextual Coherence**: The TT must be "loyal" to the ST while being "functional" for the target audience.
- **Audience Orientation**: The receiver's background, expectations, and needs are primary factors in shaping the translation.

## 2. Factors of Source-Text Analysis
Nord (2005) distinguishes between factors "outside" and "inside" the text:

### Extratextual Factors (External)
- **Sender & Intention**: Who wrote the text and what did they want to achieve?
- **Audience**: Who was the original intended receiver?
- **Medium/Channel**: How was the text transmitted (print, web, speech)?
- **Place & Time**: Where and when was the text produced?
- **Motive**: Why was the text written in the first place?

### Intratextual Factors (Internal)
- **Subject Matter & Content**: What is the text about?
- **Presuppositions**: What knowledge does the author assume the reader already has?
- **Text Composition**: The macro and micro-structure of the text.
- **Non-verbal Elements**: Images, layout, font, and other semiotic cues.
- **Lexis & Sentence Structure**: Specific word choices and grammatical patterns.
- **Suprasegmental Features**: Tone, emphasis, and rhythm (often signaled by punctuation or formatting).

## 3. The Looping Model
Translation is a recursive process:
1. **Analysis**: Looping through the ST to extract meaning and function.
2. **Transfer**: Deciding how to adapt the ST factors to the target Skopos.
3. **Synthesis**: Looping through the TT to ensure it functions correctly in the target culture.
4. **Verification**: Comparing the TT against the translation brief (the instructions provided to the translator).

## 4. Functionality plus Loyalty
- **Functionality**: The text must work as a communicative act in the target situation.
- **Loyalty**: The translator's responsibility to the source author and the target receiver, ensuring that the translation does not deceive the reader about the source's intent.
