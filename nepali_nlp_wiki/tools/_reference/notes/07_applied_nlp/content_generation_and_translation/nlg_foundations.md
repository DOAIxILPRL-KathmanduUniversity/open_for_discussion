# Natural Language Generation (NLG)

> [!IMPORTANT] Gist
> NLG is the process of producing meaningful phrases, sentences, or paragraphs from an internal representation. It is the "opposite" of Natural Language Understanding (NLU).

## 1. The Four Phases of NLG
1. **Identifying Goals**: Determining the communicative intent (what to say).
2. **Planning**: Evaluating the situation and available resources to achieve goals.
3. **Content Selection**: Selecting specific information/propositions to include.
4. **Realization**: Converting plans into actual text or voice output (syntax, idioms, words).

## 2. Key Components
- **Speaker/Application**: Initiates the process, maintains the "model of the situation," and stores history.
- **Generator**: The program/model that renders intentions into fluent, relevant phrases.

## 3. Levels of Representation
- **Textual Organization**: Ordering information sequentially and according to linguistic relations (modifications).
- **Linguistic Resources**: Choosing particular words, idioms, and syntactic constructs.
- **Realization**: The final rendering into text or speech.

## 4. NLG vs. NLU
- **NLU**: Maps text $\rightarrow$ internal representation (Decoding).
- **NLG**: Maps internal representation $\rightarrow$ text (Encoding).
