# Distant Reading & Reading at Scale

> [!IMPORTANT] Gist
> Distant reading is a method of analyzing literature by using computational tools to identify patterns across massive corpora (hundreds or thousands of texts) that would be impossible to uncover through traditional "close reading" (Walsh & Horowitz).

## 1. Close Reading vs. Distant Reading
- **Close Reading**: Deep, nuanced analysis of a single text or a few pages.
- **Distant Reading**: Aggregated analysis of trends, frequencies, and structures across a "scale" of text (Franco Moretti).
- **Cyborg Reading**: A hybrid approach where the human and computer collaborate to "read" the text (e.g., using Voyant to guide the eye toward unusual word clusters).

## 2. Tools for Scaling
- **Voyant Tools**: A web-based environment for corpus analysis (word clouds, trends, link graphs).
- **Google NGram Viewer**: Visualizing the frequency of phrases (n-grams) across the millions of books in the Google Books archive.
- **Prism**: A collaborative tool for annotating and visualizing different "readings" or "layers" of a text.

## 3. Methodologies
- **Topic Modeling (LDA)**: Discovering the underlying "topics" in a corpus by looking at which words tend to co-occur.
- **Bags of Words**: Treating a text as an unordered collection of words, ignoring grammar but preserving frequency (the basis for most statistical distant reading).
- **Sentiment Analysis in Action**: Tracking the "emotional arc" of a narrative across hundreds of novels.

## 4. Issues in Digital Text Analysis
- **OCR Errors**: Optical Character Recognition errors in digitized archives (e.g., "modern" $\to$ "modem").
- **Data Cleaning**: The "unseen labor" of removing headers, footers, and junk text before analysis.
- **Ethics of Crowdsourcing**: The role of "Prism Part Two" (Walsh & Horowitz) in using human labor to annotate large datasets.
