# Validity & Fairness in NLP

> [!IMPORTANT] Gist
> Validity and fairness are the cornerstones of ethical NLP deployment, particularly in assessment and high-stakes decision-making (Yaneva & von Davier, 2023).

## 1. Validity
- **Construct Validity**: Does the model measure what it claims to measure?
- **Response Processes**: Analyzing how the model reaches a decision (e.g., attention maps in Transformers) to ensure it's not using construct-irrelevant cues.
- **Interpretability**: The need for "explainable AI" (XAI) to support the validity of automated scores.

## 2. Fairness
- **Subgroup Fairness**: Ensuring the model performs equally well across different demographic groups (age, gender, ethnicity).
- **Bias Mitigation**: Techniques to reduce bias in training data, such as sampling strategies and data augmentation.
- **Impact Analysis**: Evaluating the real-world consequences of model decisions on human lives.

## 3. Computational Psychometrics
A blend of machine learning and psychometrics to improve item generation and scoring while maintaining scientific rigor.
