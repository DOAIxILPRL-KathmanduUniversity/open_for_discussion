# Benchmarks & Shared Tasks

> [!IMPORTANT] Gist
> Shared tasks and benchmarks drive the NLP community forward by providing standardized datasets and evaluation metrics for specific problems (Bhattacharyya & Joshi, 2023).

## 1. Shared Tasks
Standardized competitions where researchers build systems to solve a specific problem on a provided dataset.
- **SemEval (Semantic Evaluation)**: Focuses on word sense, sentiment, and semantic relations.
- **WMT (Workshop on Machine Translation)**: The premier benchmark for translation quality.
- **CoNLL Shared Tasks**: Famous for sequence labeling (NER, Chunking) and parsing.

## 2. Standard Benchmarks
- **GLUE & SuperGLUE**: Collections of NLU tasks (sentiment, entailment, similarity). SuperGLUE targets harder reasoning tasks like the **Winograd Schema Challenge**.
- **SQuAD (Stanford Question Answering Dataset)**: Reading comprehension using spans from Wikipedia paragraphs. **SQuAD 2.0** includes unanswerable questions.
- **CoQA (Conversational Question Answering)**: Focuses on interconnected questions in a dialogue flow; answers are often short (e.g., "yes/no") and context-dependent.
- **AQuA-Rat**: Mathematical reasoning through algebraic word problems, requiring models to predict both the correct answer and the **rationale**.
- **SNLI (Stanford Natural Language Inference)**: 570k sentence pairs labeled as **Entailment**, **Contradiction**, or **Neutral**.
- **iNLP Suite**: A suite specifically for Indian languages.

## 3. The Process
1. **Motivation**: Identifying a gap in current capability.
2. **Dataset Creation**: Curating and annotating a high-quality test set.
3. **Evaluation**: Defining metrics (F1, BLEU, etc.) and a leaderboard.
