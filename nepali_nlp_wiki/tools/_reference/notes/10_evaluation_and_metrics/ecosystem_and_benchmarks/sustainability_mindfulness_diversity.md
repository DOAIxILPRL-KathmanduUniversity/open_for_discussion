# Sustainability, Mindfulness & Diversity in AI

> [!IMPORTANT] Gist
> Modern machine intelligence must go beyond technical accuracy to address economic sustainability, human mindfulness, and cultural diversity (Raj et al., 2024).

## 1. Economic Sustainability
- **Efficiency**: Reducing the computational cost and energy consumption of large NLP models (Efficient Transformers).
- **Lifecycle Management**: Building software-defined machines that are self-healing and adaptive to reduce waste.

## 2. Mindfulness in AI
- **Mindful Design**: Creating interfaces that respect user attention and reduce "information overload" (HCI principles).
- **Ethical Reasoning**: Embedding AI agents with "Learning and Reasoning" frameworks that consider human values.

## 3. Diversity & Inclusion
- **Dataset Diversity**: Ensuring NLP models are trained on diverse datasets to avoid racial, gender, and linguistic bias.
- **Multilingual Support**: Prioritizing low-resource and regional languages to ensure equitable access to technology.
