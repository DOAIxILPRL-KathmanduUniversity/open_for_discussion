# Mathematical & Statistical Foundations for NLP

> [!IMPORTANT] Gist
> Modern NLP is built on the intersection of statistical learning, multivariate calculus, and linear algebra. This directory provides the rigorous theoretical scaffolding required to understand, build, and optimize high-fidelity language models (Ghayoumi, 2026; Murphy, 2012).

## 🗺️ Mathematical Roadmap

To understand the mechanics of current and future NLP architectures, explore the following foundational modules:

### 1. Core Foundations
- **[Linear Algebra & Tensors](file:///Users/paramanada/Desktop/nlp_notes_for_enthusiast/01_foundations/mathematical_and_statistical_foundations/linear_algebra_and_tensors.md)**: Vectors, matrices, SVD, and PCA. The primary data structures of deep learning.
- **[Calculus & Optimization](file:///Users/paramanada/Desktop/nlp_notes_for_enthusiast/01_foundations/mathematical_and_statistical_foundations/calculus_and_optimization.md)**: Gradients, backpropagation, and state-of-the-art optimizers (Adam, SGD).
- **[Probability & Information Theory](file:///Users/paramanada/Desktop/nlp_notes_for_enthusiast/01_foundations/mathematical_and_statistical_foundations/probability_and_information_theory.md)**: Uncertainty quantification, Bayesian inference, and Entropy.

### 2. Structural & Relational Math
- **[Graph Theory & Networks](file:///Users/paramanada/Desktop/nlp_notes_for_enthusiast/01_foundations/mathematical_and_statistical_foundations/graph_theory_and_networks.md)**: Adjacency matrices, Laplacians, and Graph Neural Networks (GNNs).
- **[Zipf's and Heaps' Laws](file:///Users/paramanada/Desktop/nlp_notes_for_enthusiast/01_foundations/mathematical_and_statistical_foundations/zipfs_law_and_heaps_law.md)**: The power-law statistical distribution of language data.

### 3. Frontier Mathematics
- **[Advanced Geometry & Topology](file:///Users/paramanada/Desktop/nlp_notes_for_enthusiast/08_modern_frontiers/future_paradigms/advanced_geometry_and_topology.md)**: Manifolds, TDA, and Riemannian geometry.
- **[Harmonic Analysis](file:///Users/paramanada/Desktop/nlp_notes_for_enthusiast/08_modern_frontiers/future_paradigms/harmonic_analysis_and_spectral_nlp.md)**: Fourier and Wavelet analysis for spectral signal processing in text.
- **[Quantum Foundations](file:///Users/paramanada/Desktop/nlp_notes_for_enthusiast/08_modern_frontiers/future_paradigms/quantum_nlp_foundations.md)**: Superposition and entanglement in compositional semantics.
