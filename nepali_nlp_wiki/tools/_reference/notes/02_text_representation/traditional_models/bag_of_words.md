# Bag of Words

## Key Ideas
- Document = unordered collection of word counts
- Sparse, high-dimensional representation
- `CountVectorizer` in scikit-learn

## Strengths & Weaknesses
- ✅ Simple, interpretable, good baseline
- ❌ Ignores word order, semantics, and context
- ❌ Vocabulary explosion on large corpora

## Variants
- Binary BoW (presence/absence)
- N-gram BoW (bigrams, trigrams)
