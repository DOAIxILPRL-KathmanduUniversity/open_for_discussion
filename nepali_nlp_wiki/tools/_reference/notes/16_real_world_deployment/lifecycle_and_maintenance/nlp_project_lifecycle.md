# NLP Project Lifecycle & KPI Alignment

> [!IMPORTANT] Gist
> Real-world NLP projects must bridge the gap between business objectives (KPIs) and technical modeling. Success is measured by both model accuracy and business outcome (Singh, 2024).

## 1. Project Phases
1. **Data Curation**: Collecting, extracting (OCR/Web Scraping), and storing text.
2. **Preprocessing**: Cleaning, normalization, and transformation into vectors (TF-IDF, Embeddings).
3. **Modeling**: Selecting between classic ML (SVM, Naive Bayes), Deep Learning (CNN, RNN), or Transformers (BERT, RoBERTa).
4. **Deployment**: Serving the model via API and integrating it into business workflows.
5. **Outcome Evaluation**: Monitoring explainability and real-world impact.

## 2. KPI Alignment
- **Business Goal**: "Reduce customer support wait times."
- **NLP Task**: "Build a goal-oriented chatbot using RASA."
- **KPI**: "Percentage of successful self-service resolutions."

## 3. Model Explainability
- **The "Why"**: Understanding why a model predicted a specific sentiment or category.
- **Bias Detection**: Identifying and mitigating gender or racial bias in embeddings and coreference models.
