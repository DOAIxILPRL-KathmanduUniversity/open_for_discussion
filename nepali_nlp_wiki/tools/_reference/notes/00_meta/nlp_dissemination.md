# NLP Dissemination: Research & Resources

> [!IMPORTANT] Gist
> Understanding where to find, read, and publish NLP research is critical for staying at the cutting edge of the field (Bhattacharyya & Joshi, 2023).

## 1. Top Venues (Conferences & Journals)
- **ACL**: Association for Computational Linguistics (The flagship conference).
- **EMNLP**: Empirical Methods in Natural Language Processing.
- **NAACL / EACL / AACL**: Regional ACL chapters.
- **Computational Linguistics**: The top journal in the field.
- **TACL**: Transactions of the Association for Computational Linguistics.

## 2. Resources for Discovery
- **ACL Anthology**: The comprehensive open-access digital archive of NLP papers.
- **arXiv (cs.CL)**: Preprint server for the latest research before official publication.
- **Papers with Code**: Tracks SOTA performance on various NLP benchmarks.

## 3. Publication Process
1. **Drafting**: Scholarly writing with mathematical grounding and experimental validation.
2. **Peer Review**: Anonymous review by experts in the sub-field.
3. **Camera-Ready**: Final version after incorporating reviewer feedback.
